# Validation Checklist

## Automated Validation

- [ ] Required tests were run.
- [ ] Test output or check links are recorded.
- [ ] Failures are explained or resolved.

## Manual Validation

- [ ] Manual test steps are listed.
- [ ] Checklist is specific enough for the human reviewer to act without re-reading the full directive.
- [ ] Manual results are recorded by an authorized human reviewer.
- [ ] Gaps are explicitly marked.

## Physical Validation

- [ ] Physical validation is required or explicitly not applicable.
- [ ] Physical results are recorded by an authorized human reviewer.
- [ ] Automated tests are not treated as physical validation.

## Human Approval

- [ ] Required approval gate is named.
- [ ] Approver is recorded.
- [ ] Evidence reference is recorded.
